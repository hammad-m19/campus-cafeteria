Answers to everything you need to know about Campus Bites

Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash (PKR), JazzCash, Easypaisa, and student card balance. Debit and credit cards are currently not supported, but we are working on adding card payment terminals soon. For mobile payments, simply show the QR code at the billing counter.

How does the token system work?

After you pay at the counter, you receive a numbered token slip. Head to the seating area and wait for your number to be called out (and displayed on the screen). When your number appears, go to the pick-up counter with your token to collect your order. The average wait is 5 to 10 minutes.

Do you accommodate food allergies?

Yes! Please inform our counter staff about any allergies before ordering. Common allergens like nuts, dairy, and gluten are present in some items. We do our best to accommodate requests, but our kitchen handles multiple allergens so cross-contamination is possible. Items marked with allergy badges on the menu board can help you choose safely.

What are your peak hours and when should I visit for shorter waits?

Our busiest times are 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM (lunch rush) and 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM. For the shortest waits, visit between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM, or between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM. During exam weeks, we extend hours and the evening shift (7:00 PM to 11:00 PM) tends to be calm.

Can I get a refund if my order is wrong?

Absolutely. If your order is incorrect or you are unsatisfied, bring it back to the pick-up counter immediately with your token. We will either remake your order or issue a full refund. Please report any issues within 10 minutes of receiving your food. Our goal is your complete satisfaction.

Do you accept pre-orders or advance orders?

Yes, we accept pre-orders for groups of 10 or more people. Contact us via WhatsApp at +92 321 1234567 at least 24 hours in advance. Pre-orders are great for study group sessions, departmental events, or class celebrations. A 50% advance payment is required for pre-orders.

Is there a student discount?

Our Student Combo deal is specifically priced for students, and you need a valid student ID to avail it. Additionally, during exam weeks, we offer a flat 10% discount on all items for students showing their university ID card. Faculty and staff also receive a 10% courtesy discount.

Do you have vegetarian and halal options?

All our meat is 100% halal certified. We source from verified halal suppliers and maintain strict halal standards in our kitchen. For vegetarian options, look for the green "Vegetarian" badge on menu items. We offer several vegetarian choices including Daal Chawal, Halwa Puri, Loaded Fries (without meat toppings), and Gulab Jamun.

Can I host an event or party at Campus Bites?

We love hosting campus events! Our space can accommodate small gatherings of up to 25 people for birthday celebrations, farewell parties, or departmental meetups. For larger events, we can set up a catering arrangement. Contact us at hello@campusbites.pk or WhatsApp +92 321 1234567 to discuss your needs and get a custom quote.
