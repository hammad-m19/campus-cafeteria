👥 About Campus Bites

The story behind your favorite campus kitchen

🍳

Our Story

Campus Bites started in 2018 as a small food stall in the corner of Block C at the National University of Technology. Founded by Chef Farhan and his wife Nadia, the idea was simple: serve honest, home-style food at prices that students could actually afford. What started as a two-person operation with a single paratha griddle and a chai pot has grown into the most loved dining spot on campus.

Today, we serve over 500 meals daily across four food categories, and our Doodh Patti Chai has become legendary. But our core values remain the same: fresh ingredients, generous portions, and a warm smile with every order.

Our Journey

From a humble stall to the heart of campus dining

2018 - The Beginning

A Small Stall, Big Dreams

Chef Farhan sets up a modest food stall in Block C with a limited menu of paratha rolls and chai. Word spreads quickly among students.

2019 - Growing Fast

Expanded Menu and Seating

We added fast food items, biryani, and desserts. The university provided us with a proper indoor space seating 40 guests.

2021 - Digital Token System

Modernizing the Experience

Introduced the token-based ordering system to reduce wait times and improve flow during peak lunch hours.

2023 - Award Winning

Best Campus Eatery Award

Voted "Best Campus Eatery" by the Student Council for the second consecutive year. Launched combo deals and the Student Discount Program.

2026 - Today

Serving 500+ Meals Daily

A team of 12, a full kitchen, a dedicated pastry section, and plans for a second outlet at the Engineering Block. The journey continues!

Mission and Values

What drives us every single day

🌿

Freshness First

Every ingredient is sourced fresh daily from local markets. We never compromise on quality or use pre-packaged shortcuts.

💰

Affordable for All

We believe every student deserves a good meal. Our pricing is designed to be accessible without sacrificing taste or quality.

🤝

Community Spirit

Campus Bites is more than a cafeteria. It is a gathering place where ideas are born, friendships are made, and memories are shared.

🌱

Sustainability

We minimize waste through smart portioning, composting, and partnering with campus sustainability initiatives.
