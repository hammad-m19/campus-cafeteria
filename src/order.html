📋 How to Order

Four simple steps from craving to eating

Follow These Steps

Ordering at Campus Bites is quick and easy

1 📝

Choose Your Items

Browse the menu board above the counter or check our menu page. Pick your main, sides, and drinks. Our staff is happy to help with recommendations!

2 💳

Pay at the Counter

Head to the billing counter and place your order. We accept cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and student card balance. Confirm your total and pay.

3 🎫

Collect Your Token

After payment, you will receive a numbered token slip. Keep it safe - your token number will be called when your food is ready.

4 🍴

Pick Up Your Order

Listen for your token number at the pick-up counter. Show your token, grab your tray, and enjoy your meal! Bon appetit!

📜 Pick-Up Rules

A few things to keep in mind

  • 🎫
    Keep your token. You must present your token slip to collect your order. Lost tokens require manager verification.
  • ⏰
    Average wait: 5-10 minutes. During peak hours (11 AM - 1 PM), orders may take up to 15 minutes.
  • 📣
    Listen for your number. Token numbers are called out loud and displayed on the screen above the pick-up counter.
  • 📦
    Takeaway available. Let the cashier know if you want your order packed for takeaway. No extra charge.
  • ✅
    Check your order. Please verify all items before leaving the counter. We are happy to fix any errors on the spot.
  • ♾
    Accessibility. Priority service is available for students with disabilities. Just inform the counter staff.
